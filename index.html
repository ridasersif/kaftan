Our Heritage

The story of KaftanLhama

Founded in the heart of Casablanca, KaftanLhama is more than a fashion house; it is a sanctuary for Moroccan artisanal heritage. We believe that every stitch tells a story of tradition, elegance, and mastery.

Our master tailors (Maâlems) spend hundreds of hours on a single piece, ensuring that the legacy of Moroccan luxury lives on in every garment we create.
